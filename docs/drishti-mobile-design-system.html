01 · Identity

The brand

Drishti (Sanskrit दृष्टि, "vision / insight") renames the product with a single, ownable idea: the app is the lens that resolves a raw database into something a person can actually see and understand. Every agent, chart, and report serves that one promise.

The mark — an iris / aperture

Concentric "vision rings" closing onto a luminous bindu (center point) — at once an eye, a camera aperture, and a data-lens focusing. It draws itself in on launch, then breathes with a soft glow. Works as a favicon, a loading state, and the "Ask" button.

Product name is Sanskrit; feature labels stay crisp English for usability, with Manthan kept as the one evocative feature name — the agents "churn" your data to surface insight.

Voice

Confident, plain, and specific — never salesy. The app says exactly what happens ("Churning 4 tables…", then "Found it"). Numbers before adjectives. Errors explain the fix. Hindi/Sanskrit shows up only as accents (the wordmark, Manthan), never as friction.

03 · Iconography

08 · Layout & interaction rules

Fit, scroll, zoom, gesture

The patterns every screen obeys so content always fits a phone and nothing feels arbitrary.

01
Fixed frame, scrolling body.

The status bar, app bar, and bottom tab bar are always fixed. Only the content region between them scrolls — so navigation is never lost and the "Ask" FAB is always one tap away.

02
Horizontal scroll for toolbars & wide data.

Editor toolbars, slicer chips, and any wide table scroll sideways inside their own track — the page body never scrolls horizontally. A fading edge hints "there's more".

03
Charts fit small, zoom big.

On a card, a chart renders compact and legible (compact axis numbers, angled labels, tooltips). Tap Expand → full-screen with pinch-zoom & pan. Never rely on reading a dense chart at card size.

04
Long-press = context, swipe = quick action.

Long-press a widget/board/message opens its action sheet; swipe a list row reveals delete/share; pull-to-refresh re-runs live queries. Consistent everywhere.

05
Sheets over new screens for focused tasks.

Edit, filter-builder, analysis, annotations, and the report copilot are bottom sheets — keep context visible behind them. Full navigation only for changing destination.

06
Never blank, always honest.

Loading = skeleton shimmer; empty = a clear prompt + primary action; an unsupported chart degrades to a labeled table, never a blank box; errors state the fix. A widget always shows something.

07
Safe areas & thumb reach.

Respect the notch/home-indicator insets; primary actions sit in the lower third (FAB, sheet buttons) where the thumb lives; destructive actions never sit under a resting thumb.

08
Reduced-motion is first-class.

Every animation checks prefers-reduced-motion; when set, reveals become instant fades and ambient loops stop — the app stays fully usable.
